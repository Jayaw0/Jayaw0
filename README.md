# Hi, I’m Jaya 👋

I work on building and understanding **large language model (LLM) systems** in real world settings.

My interests sit at the intersection of **LLM behavior**, **system reliability**, and **practical deployment** especially how models reason, fail, and improve once they move beyond demos.

---

## 🧠 What I Care About

- How LLMs behave under real constraints  
- Evaluation beyond accuracy: reliability, consistency, failure modes  
- Designing systems that validate, reason, and recover  
- Bridging experimentation with production reality  

I enjoy working on problems where **clean abstractions meet messy data**.

---

## 🔬 Current Focus

- Studying and prototyping LLM systems with an emphasis on evaluation 
- Studying hallucinations, reasoning errors, and model behavior  
- Designing evaluation and validation layers around models  
- Exploring system-level approaches to make LLMs more dependable
